A Letter to Mumma

11th May 2025

My Dearest Mumma,

There aren't enough words in any language to describe how much you mean to me. But today, I'm going to say something.

You are the reason I laugh louder, dream bigger, and feel braver. Every single thing I am — the good parts, especially — is because of you. Your hands have held mine through every storm, and your eyes have always seen the best version of me, even when I couldn't see it myself.

Thank you for every early morning, every late night, every meal made with love, every hug that fixed everything. Thank you for being the person I can always run home to.

You are not just my mother — you are my favourite person in the whole world. My safe place. My biggest fan. My home.

I hope today and every day forward, you feel even a fraction of the joy and love you've poured into my life. You deserve all the flowers, all the sunshine, all the happiness.

I love you more than you'll ever know, Mumma. Today, tomorrow, always. 💛

Forever yours,
Annu 🌻

You are loved,
Mumma.

Now. Always. Unconditionally. ✨
